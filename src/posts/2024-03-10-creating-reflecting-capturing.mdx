I've been writing more lately, and it's a big reason I want this site up and running—to have a place to **reflect out loud**, connect ideas, and share what I'm learning.

I've also started using Obsidian more seriously. Inspired by [Sam Altman's note-taking approach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tDmjz6HB-yw), I'm building a second brain that helps me revisit thoughts, track questions, and generate better ideas.
